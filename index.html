About your tutor

I'm Aysan Rahimi. I studied mathematics education, then went on to a master's degree in soft computing, where I work on algorithms and AI. Before moving into data science, I spent eight years teaching in the classroom.

That mix shapes how I tutor. I explain the idea first, show where the formula comes from, and only then ask you to practise. If a topic is shaky, we spend the time there instead of rushing to the next chapter.
